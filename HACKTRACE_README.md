HACKTRACE - MarcusEthan

Objective
HACKTRACE - MarcusEthan was created to raise awareness about how cyber attacks can happen in everyday websites. 
The main goal was to help people understand how attackers can gather information just by exploring a public-facing portfolio.
The event used a fake personal website for a character named Ethan Marcus. Participants were asked to explore the site and find hidden clues. These clues, when put together, revealed a password—showing how small details can sometimes lead to security risks. The project is mainly used in learning environments to give people hands-on experience with information gathering techniques.

Outcome
During the event, participants interacted with the Ethan Marcus portfolio website. They had to carefully browse, hover, and inspect parts of the site to discover hidden hints. These clues were placed in unexpected areas like tooltips, code comments, and animations.
As players explored the site, they began thinking like ethical hackers—checking the site’s behavior, looking into files, and paying attention to small details. Some found the password quickly; others learned by trial and error. This realistic setup helped participants understand how real-world hackers might approach their targets.

Key Takeaway
The event successfully showed:
. How attackers gather information without breaking into anything.
. Why small design choices in websites can lead to leaks.
. The value of curiosity and attention to detail when testing for vulnerabilities.

Many participants finished the challenge with a better understanding of how websites can unintentionally give away clues. It also gave them practical experience in thinking like a cybersecurity analyst.
